Digital Arrest & Police Video Call Extortion

Scammers dress in fake police/CBI uniforms over Skype/WhatsApp video calls, alleging illegal parcels with your Aadhaar, demanding fund transfers.

  • 🛡️ Rule: Cut call immediately. Police never fine you via video.

  • ⚠️ Defense: Establish a secret family passphrase.

Financial Trap

UPI QR Code "Scan to Receive Money" Trap

Scammers pose as buyers on OLX or cashback portals and send a QR code claiming: "Scan to receive payment in your bank". Entering your PIN debits your money.

  • ⚠️ NPCI Rule: UPI PIN is ONLY needed to SEND money, never to RECEIVE.
  • 🛡️ Rule: Never scan a QR code to accept cash.

🚨 CRITICAL ALERT

1. Digital Arrest & Police Video Call Extortion

Modus Operandi: Fraudsters call claiming an illegal parcel containing narcotics or foreign currency was intercepted in your name by Customs or FedEx. They transfer your call to "CBI Officers" via Skype video call, showing staged police stations and fake Supreme Court arrest warrants.

  • 🛑 Fact: Neither Supreme Court, Police, CBI, nor RBI ever arrests or interrogates citizens over video call.
  • 🔐 Action: Disconnect immediately. Block the caller. Report to 1930 and Chakshu (sancharsaathi.gov.in).
Average extortion: ₹5L - ₹2 Cr Report 1930 ↗
⚠️ APK MALWARE

2. India Post / Courier "Address Incomplete" APK Scam

Modus Operandi: You receive an SMS: "India Post: Your package cannot be delivered due to incomplete address. Update address within 24 hours at [malicious-link.apk]". Installing the file grants Accessibility and SMS permissions, allowing hackers to silently read bank OTPs.

  • 🛑 Fact: Postal services never send .apk files via SMS for address updates.
  • 🔐 Action: Never sideload APKs from WhatsApp or SMS. Use official postal tracking websites only.
Trojan SMS Stealer Report 1930 ↗
📉 INVESTMENT FRAUD

3. VIP WhatsApp & Telegram Institutional Trading Scam

Modus Operandi: Victims are invited into "VIP Stock Advisory Groups" claiming affiliations with top institutional funds. They show fake trading app dashboards reflecting 300% fictitious profits, but demand huge "tax fees" when you attempt to withdraw.

  • 🛑 Fact: SEBI-registered brokers NEVER solicit retail investors through WhatsApp or Telegram groups.
  • 🔐 Action: Check the SEBI official registry before investing any money.
₹800+ Cr Lost in 2025-26 Report 1930 ↗
🤖 AI AUDIO FRAUD

4. AI Voice Cloning & Fake Accident / Bail Extortion

Modus Operandi: Scammers scrape a short video clip of your son or daughter from Instagram, feed it to neural voice clone models, and call parents: "Papa, police caught me in an accident! Send ₹50,000 for bail now!"

  • 🛑 Fact: AI voices replicate tone but fail on personal shared memories.
  • 🔐 Action: Ask a family secret code word or call your relative's personal number directly.
Psychological Extortion Report 1930 ↗
💳 UPI TRAP

5. "Scan QR to Receive Money / Cashback" UPI Trap

Modus Operandi: Fraudsters posing as OLX buyers or government cashback agents send a QR code with text like "Scan & Enter PIN to Receive ₹15,000". Once you input your UPI PIN, money is debited from your account.

  • 🛑 Golden Rule: UPI PIN is ONLY entered to DEBIT money from your bank account.
  • 🔐 Action: Receiving money NEVER requires entering your UPI PIN or scanning a code.
Most Prevalent UPI Scam Report 1930 ↗
⚡ UTILITY PHISHING

6. Electricity Bill Disconnection & Smart Meter SMS

Modus Operandi: Urgent SMS at 8 PM: "Dear Customer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Call Electricity Officer at 98xxxxxx immediately". Calling leads to remote-desktop app installs (AnyDesk/TeamViewer).

  • 🛑 Fact: Electricity distribution boards send formal notices, not random mobile number SMS.
  • 🔐 Action: Pay only through official state electricity portals or verified consumer apps.
Remote Screen Takeover Report 1930 ↗

Generative AI & Deepfake Awareness

How modern fraudsters weaponize voice synthesis, face swapping, and AI generation to deceive families and businesses.

🤖 2026 Threat Intelligence

Understanding 3-Second Audio Cloning

Modern neural models can clone anyone's voice from a 3-second audio sample scraped from Instagram stories, TikTok reels, or YouTube videos. Criminals then call parents pretending to be their child in custody or after an accident.

🛡️ Essential Protocol: Establish a confidential family safe-word to immediately verify distress calls.

🚨 1. Establish a Family Safe-Word

Agree on an unguessable secret passphrase with your family (parents, children, spouse). If anyone calls claiming an accident or police custody asking for money, demand the passphrase. An AI bot or scammer will never know it.

👁️ 2. Spot Video Call Deepfake Artifacts

Ask the caller to turn their head sideways or wave their hand in front of their face. Current real-time video face-swapping algorithms will glitch, blur around the nose/ears, and reveal distorted facial borders.

📞 3. The 60-Second Direct Callback Rule

Scammers rely on extreme emotional panic. Hang up immediately, wait 30 seconds, and call the relative back on their direct saved phone number or WhatsApp video. Never transfer funds before direct confirmation.

Why Passphrases Beat Complex Passwords

Instead of hard-to-remember passwords like P@$$w0rd! (which hackers crack in seconds), use a 4-word random passphrase like Solar-Tiger-Coffee-Orbit!. It takes over 2.4 million years to crack via brute-force attack!

🛡️ Recommendation: Use an open-source password manager like Bitwarden or 1Password with 2FA enabled.

Social Media Privacy & Identity Protection

Practical checklists to lock down Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and protect your digital footprint.

Privacy Control

Instagram & Facebook Profile Hardening

Switch your account to Private, turn off location metadata (geotagging) on camera apps, and disable search engine indexing so identity thieves cannot harvest family photos.

Login Security

WhatsApp Two-Step Verification (PIN)

Enable WhatsApp's 6-digit PIN under Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification. This prevents fraudsters from hijacking your WhatsApp even if they succeed in a SIM swap attack.

Identity Theft

Clone Profile & DM Extortion

Criminals create identical profiles using your profile photo and send DMs to your friends asking for urgent money. Never vote in unverified online contests or click links in DMs.
